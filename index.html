This is a fox! His name is Fred.

Below are the 12 "true" foxes, all belonging to the genus Vulpes of the subfamily Caninae (sound familiar? yes foxes belong to the same subfamily as dogs!).

Lets start with the fox you think of when you hear "fox". The default, regular fox.

However, let's call this fox by its actual name -- the red fox! Fred is a red fox.

1. Red Fox

legal name: Vulpes vulpes

You can find the red fox pretty much anywhere, from forests and grasslands to tundras and deserts. You might even find an urban red fox.

2. Swift Fox

legal name: Vulpes velox

Smaller or equal to the size of a house cat, this fox is among the smallest foxes in the world. The swift fox is called swift for a reason -- it can run up to 60 kilometres per hour, double the speed of a car in residential and school zones (30 kilometres per hour speed limit).
